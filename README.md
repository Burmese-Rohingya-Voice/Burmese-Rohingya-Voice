## 🎙️ Voice recording for AI

Consent-based voice data for text-to-speech (TTS), speech recognition (ASR) and voice assistants.

- Scripted and spontaneous speech
- Speakers of different ages, genders and dialects
- Clean audio files with a metadata sheet for every speaker
- Transcription and quality checks on every batch
- Written consent and licensing for every speaker (see [CONSENT_FORM.md](CONSENT_FORM.md))

## 🏷️ Annotation and transcription

- Audio and video transcription with timestamps and speaker labels
- Data labeling: intent, sentiment, entities, quality ratings
- Quality review against your project guidelines

## 🌐 Translation

- English ↔ Burmese, Rohingya and Hindi
- Documents, UI strings, prompts, health and education material

## 🏥 Interpreting for facilities and LEP individuals

Language access for people with limited English proficiency (LEP):

- **Healthcare:** intake, appointments, consent forms, medication instructions, mental health support
- **Schools:** parent meetings, enrollment, child welfare
- **NGOs and humanitarian groups:** case management, outreach, distributions
- **Social services:** applications, benefits, housing and resettlement support

Remote or on-site. Accurate, impartial and confidential.

## ⚖️ Ethics and confidentiality

- Every voice recording is made with **informed written consent**
- Speakers know how their voice will be used, including AI training or voice cloning
- Voices are used only for the agreed purpose
- Client data and recordings are never published
- Medical and personal information stays confidential
